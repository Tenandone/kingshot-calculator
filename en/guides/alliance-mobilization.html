Alliance Mobilization: Choosing Missions and Saving Materials

Compare mission requirements, completion time and refresh costs in Alliance Mobilization while protecting speedups and development materials on free-to-play and low-spend accounts.

Set your participation goal before chasing points

Alliance Mobilization offers personal and alliance rewards through completed missions. For a free-to-play or low-spend account, check how much your planned development and normal play can complete before choosing the largest point value. Compare this event's rewards with the materials you want to retain for your next development goal.

At the start, read the current participation and reward requirements, then check your alliance's expectations. Set your goal from the missions displayed this round rather than memorizing the counts or points in an older season's table.

Five checks before accepting a mission

  1. Read which action earns progress. Matching icons do not establish that the action, required amount and reward points are identical.
  2. Check whether you can finish within the time limit. Having enough materials is different from having enough time to log in.
  3. Calculate what your stock and already planned work can cover. Extra speedups, Gems or packs add to the mission's cost.
  4. Check whether you would consume materials reserved for Hall of Governors or KvK preparation. Do not overlook the shortfall this creates for your next goal.
  5. Check whether you must accept the mission before making progress and whether rewards need to be claimed after completion. Confirm its current status before acting.

The same mission can suit different accounts differently

When planned training can complete the mission

For a troop-training mission, check your current training queue and expected completion time first. If the planned training matches the required action and can finish without extra speedups before the deadline, it is worth comparing with your other options. Read whether training or promotion counts and whether the requirement is completion or the use of a particular item.

When a high score requires unplanned spending

If a research mission would consume all your remaining speedups, or an equipment mission would bring forward an upgrade you meant to postpone, compare the reward with the delay to your next goal. A lower-point mission that fits your stock and schedule may be a better choice. Conversely, there is no need to reject every material-consuming mission if it matches development you had already planned.

When another event overlaps

Do not spend speedups simply because construction, research or training events appear on the same schedule. First check whether both events count the same action and whether their scoring windows actually overlap. Use the overlap when planned work can serve both goals, and check whether chasing the extra reward increases your total consumption. The Hall of Governors guide can help you plan your next material-use window.

Decide whether to refresh or wait

If the available missions do not suit you, check the displayed refresh cost and waiting time. Also read the rules for abandoning an accepted mission before doing so. Repeatedly refreshing in the hope of a higher score can consume more Gems or time than you intended.

  • If a mission is achievable, compare its additional cost as well as its point value.
  • If none currently fits, see whether you have time to wait for a free refresh or your next login.
  • Set a budget before paying to refresh, and reconsider when the next cost is displayed.
  • Near the end, prioritize what you can finish in the remaining time over the ideal mission you hope to find.

What to coordinate with your alliance

R4 and R5 members can review which shared missions are difficult for the alliance to complete, then communicate refresh permissions and selection rules based on the current event. Tell members about upcoming events that need saved materials as well as participation targets. Letting each account choose achievable missions makes planning easier than expecting the same spending from everyone.

Final checks

Before the event ends, review the remaining time and completion status of active missions and whether personal and alliance rewards have been claimed. Afterwards, record the speedups, Gems and development materials spent alongside your points. Including what you kept for the next event gives you a more useful comparison of participation costs for the next round.
