Lynx

The Lynx is a Generation 1 pet that can be upgraded up to level 60. Max refinement: 17.4%. Unlock after 54 days and Town Center LV.18

This page summarizes Lynx's skill effect, total upgrade materials, and the pet calculator link in one place.

Main Use
Stamina Recovery

Lynx is one of the early pets that helps you manage stamina more efficiently. Her skill becomes especially useful when you want to keep hunting, gathering rewards, or continue daily activity without waiting too long.

Skill Effect

Comforting Embrace
Felines of all stripes have the ability to soothe a Governor’s mind, immediately restoring 60 Stamina. (Cooldown: 23h)

Upgrade Preview

Governor Stamina Amount: 35 / 40 / 45 / 50 / 55 / 60

As Lynx advances, her instant stamina recovery increases from 35 to 60. This makes her a practical pet when you want more flexibility during daily activity.

Lynx FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about Lynx.

When does Lynx unlock in Kingshot?

Lynx is a Gen 1 pet and usually becomes available around day 55 of the server.

What is the max level of Lynx?

Lynx can be leveled up to Level 60.

What does Lynx's skill do?

Lynx uses Comforting Embrace to instantly restore Governor Stamina. Depending on advancement level, the amount increases from 35 to 60.

Where can I calculate the exact upgrade materials?

You can use the Kingshot pet calculator to check the exact materials needed from your current level to your target level.
