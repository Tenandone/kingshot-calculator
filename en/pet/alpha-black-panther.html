Main Use
Troop Lethality Buff

Alpha Black Panther is a Generation 5 battle pet built to increase offensive power. Its active skill raises troop lethality for a long combat window, making it useful for players who want stronger troop damage in battle.

Skill Effect

Deadly Bite
The ferocious black panther attacks its target relentlessly, increasing Troop Lethality by 10.00% for 2 hours. (Cooldown: 20 hours)

Upgrade Preview

Troop Lethality Increase: 2.5% / 3.0% / 3.5% / 4.0% / 5.0% / 6.0% / 7.0% / 8.0% / 9.0% / 10.0%

As Alpha Black Panther advances, the lethality bonus steadily grows. This gives the pet strong value for battle-focused players who want to improve their offensive troop performance over a two-hour duration.

Alpha Black Panther FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about Alpha Black Panther.

When does Alpha Black Panther unlock in Kingshot?

Alpha Black Panther is a Generation 5 pet in Kingshot that becomes available when your kingdom is around 270 days old, give or take.

What is the max level of Alpha Black Panther?

Alpha Black Panther can be leveled up to Level 100.

What does Alpha Black Panther's skill do?

Alpha Black Panther uses Deadly Bite to increase Troop Lethality for 2 hours. At the highest upgrade level, the buff reaches 10.00%.
