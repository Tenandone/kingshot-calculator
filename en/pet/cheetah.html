Cheetah

The Cheetah is a Generation 2 pet that can be upgraded up to level 70. Max refinement: 26%. Unlock after 90 days and Bison LV.15

Pet Food Supply

Cheetah is a useful Generation 2 pet for players who want a steady source of pet food. His skill gives free pet food on a fixed cooldown, making long-term pet progression a little easier.

Skill Effect

Scent Mastery
As a natural predator, its keen sense of smell allows it to instantly locate 500 pet food. (Cooldown: 23h)

Upgrade Preview

Pet Food: 200 / 250 / 300 / 350 / 400 / 450 / 500

As Cheetah advances, the amount of pet food gained from the skill increases steadily. This makes him valuable if you want more consistent pet growth materials over time.

Cheetah FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about Cheetah.

When does Cheetah unlock in Kingshot?

Cheetah is a Generation 2 pet in Kingshot that becomes available when your state is around 80 days old.

What is the max level of Cheetah?

Cheetah can be leveled up to Level 70.

What does Cheetah's skill do?

Cheetah uses Scent Mastery to instantly locate pet food. At the highest upgrade level, the skill gives 500 pet food every 23 hours.

Where can I calculate the exact upgrade materials?

You can use the Kingshot pet calculator to check the exact materials needed from your current level to your target level.
