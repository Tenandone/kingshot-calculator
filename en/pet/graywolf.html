Gray Wolf

The Gray Wolf is a Generation 1 pet that can be upgraded up to level 50. Max refinement: 8.70%. Unlock after 54 days and Town Center LV.18

Main Role
Construction Speed

Gray Wolf is one of the early utility pets that can help speed up city growth. If you are focusing on faster building progression, this pet is worth checking early.

Skill Effect

The wolf silently yet cleverly delivers tools to the yard, increasing Construction Speed by 15.00% for 5 minutes. (Cooldown: 23h)

Upgrade Preview:

Construction Speedup: 5.00% / 7.00% / 9.00% / 12.00% / 15.00%

This makes Gray Wolf a practical pet for players who want to shorten building time during key upgrade windows.

Gray Wolf FAQ

Quick answers for common Gray Wolf questions

When does Gray Wolf unlock in Kingshot?

Gray Wolf usually becomes available around day 55 of the server.

What is the max level of Gray Wolf?

Gray Wolf can be upgraded up to Level 50.

Is Gray Wolf good for construction?

Yes. Gray Wolf is a construction-focused pet and helps improve building speed during timed usage.
