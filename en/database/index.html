Using this data

Growth materials belong to different systems. Use the governor gear and charm tables for equipment stages, and the shard table for hero stars. Hero gear enhancement and exclusive widgets are separate systems. To total a range, open the matching calculator and enter your current stage; exclude materials already spent from your inventory.
