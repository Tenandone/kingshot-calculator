Effects & details

Overview

The Guard Station is a defensive building unlocked at Town Center level 7. It helps protect your city from enemy attacks. If your city is attacked and defeated, it will start burning, causing the Guard Station to lose health over time. Additional attacks extend the burning duration, which makes the Guard Station lose even more health.

When the Guard Station reaches 0 HP, your city will be teleported to a random location on the map. This is especially important in events like Swordland Showdown and Viking Vengeance.

For better defense, assign your top three heroes to the Guard Station, or use heroes with defender skills.

Restoring the Guard Station

After an attack, you can restore some Guard Station health every 30 minutes. You can’t instantly restore it to full health with gems—your restoration is limited by the 30-minute cooldown.

If you’re online during an attack, you can use gems to extinguish the fire. This prevents the Guard Station from losing more health and helps you avoid getting teleported.

Repair steps

  • Go to the Guard Station.
  • Press City Defense.
  • If damaged, press Repair Guard Station to restore health.
  • Repeat every 30 minutes until fully repaired.
