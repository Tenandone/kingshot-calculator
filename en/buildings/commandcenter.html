All levels, costs, capacities, and upgrade times for Command Center

Rally Capacity vs. Troop Deployment Capacity

  • Rally Capacity is the total troops a rally can hold. Rallies are alliance group attacks (e.g., terrors, events).
  • Troop Deployment Capacity is how many troops you can send in solo marches (beasts, certain events, etc.).

How to Calculate Troop Deployment Capacity

  • Base value: 500 troops
  • Add the amount provided by your Command Center
  • Add the boost from the Academy
  • Add the Troop Deployment Capacity of each of the three heroes in your march
  • Add any extra bonuses from City Bonuses

How to Calculate Rally Capacity

  • Base value: 2,000
  • Add the Rally Capacity provided by your Command Center
  • Add other bonuses (events, city bonuses, etc.) if applicable
