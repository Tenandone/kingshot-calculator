This is one of your Alliance buildings. The purpose of it is to have a place to keep Reinforcements that are sent to you by Alliance members. It is also what dictates the amount of help you can get from your Alliance members when it comes to Construction, Research, and Healing. Upgrading this building is typically a requirement to upgrade your Town Center to the Next level. Upgrading the Embassy increases the total amount of Reinforcements your Alliance can send you, the amount of times your Alliance members can help you speed up Construction/Research/ Healing, and boosts your power.

Effects and conditions

Upgrading the Embassy improves three important stats

  • Ally Assists: How many times alliance members can help speed up your upgrades, research, or troop healing.
  • Ally Help Time: Seconds reduced from an ally’s upgrade/research/healing when you assist them.
  • Reinforce Capacity: Maximum troops you can receive as reinforcements to defend your city.
Note: Resource costs can vary with heroes/research buffs. Values shown here are raw/base values.
