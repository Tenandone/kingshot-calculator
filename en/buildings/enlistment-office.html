When the number of injured soldiers exceeds the capacity limit of the Infirmary, the Enlistment Office will recruit reserves to replace some of the soldiers you've lost in battle. Recruit from your reserve by expending Loyalty and it will begin to re-accumulate. Factors affecting the Loyalty rate: - The lower your current Loyalty, the faster it will accumulate. - The fewer the severely injured in the Infirmary, the faster it will accumulate. - The more reserves (excluding reserves that can be enlisted immediately) in the Enlistment Office, the faster it will accumulate. Please note, Loyalty will stop increasing after reaching a certain limit. You can obtain Lovalty by collecting rewards from daily missions or gathering if the reserve in the Enlistment Office is not empty. You will get more as your Town Center level Increases. The Enlistment Office capacity is 400% of the Infirmary’s capacity.
