Barracks

The building is used to train and upgrade Infantry. 7.

Read more

The building is used to train and upgrade Infantry. Governors can unlock the building at Town Center Lv.7. They upgrade to the level of your Town Center and are usually one of the upgrade requirements needed to upgrade your Town Center to the Next level. Each upgrade increases the Training Capacity (how many troops you can train at one time), reduces training time, and boosts your power. At certain upgrade levels, you will gain access to higher tier troops. Once you have unlocked T5 troops, you will gain the ability to promote your lower tier troops to the highest trainable tier. Researching the technology Camp Expansion in the Growth tab of the Academy will further increase the number of troops you can train at one time. Researching Training Tools, also in the Growth tab, decreases the time needed to train a group of troops.

This building is used to train and upgrade True Gold Infantry. Governors can unlock the building when their Town Center have reached True Gold level. They upgrade to the level of your Town Center and are usually one of the upgrade requirements needed to upgrade your Town Center to the Next level. Each upgrade increases the Training Capacity(how many troops you can train at one time), reduces training time, and boosts your power. Researching the technology Camp Expansion in the Growth tab of the Research Center will further increase the number of troops you can train at one time. Researching Training Tools, also in the Growth tab, decreases the time needed to train a group of troops.

Overview

The Barracks is unlocked at Town Center level 7. It is one of the core progression buildings: Infantry tiers unlock at specific Barracks levels, and Training Capacity/Speed grow as you upgrade.
