Truegold Crucible

The Truegold Crucible unlocks when your kingdom reaches Truegold Level 5 , which often happens around day ~150 of the kingdom’s age (varies by server).

Effects & detailsRelated buildings

No upgrade table is currently available.

Effects & details

Overview

This building lets you convert normal resources (Bread, Wood, Stone, and Iron — all four at once) into a small amount of Truegold by performing a refinement.

Note: Unlock timing and daily refinement limits can vary by server age and game updates. Use the tables below as a practical reference.

Truegold Refinement Chances

Each refinement gives a random Truegold amount based on these rates.

Result Chance
1 Truegold40%
2 Truegold30%
3 Truegold15%
4 Truegold10%
5 Truegold5%

On average, you can expect to earn around 10.5 Truegold per day from the Truegold Crucible.

Refinement Limits & Costs

You can do 5 refinements per day during the early stages of Truegold (Level 5 era). As your kingdom ages more, the number of daily refinements increases.

Each refinement costs more resources than the last (Bread / Wood / Stone / Iron each time):

Refinement Cost (Bread / Wood / Stone / Iron)
1st5,000 each
2nd10,000 each
3rd20,000 each
4th30,000 each
5th40,000 each

Total cost of 5 refinements: 105,000 of each resource.

Other Info

  • The Truegold Crucible is free to build — no resources needed.
  • It can be built instantly when your kingdom hits Truegold Level 5.
  • It has no level upgrades.
